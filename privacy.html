AIHelper Privacy Policy

This policy describes AIHelper for iPhone, provided by Heng Zhang.

Developer data collection

AIHelper does not operate a developer-hosted account, chat, analytics, advertising or tracking service. We do not receive your conversations, photos, pairing tokens, model credentials or device identifiers through an AIHelper developer server. There are no advertising or analytics SDKs in the app.

Local data

The app stores paired Bridge connection details (including the pairing token), cached session information and preferences on your device to support its functions. These are not uploaded to us. Remove a saved Bridge using its delete action. Remove the app and its data to clear its local storage. Device backups may retain app data according to your Apple device and backup settings.

Messages and attachments

When you use a live connection, messages and the photos you select are sent to your paired Mac Bridge. Your Mac execution owner may send them to a model provider or use tools according to the accounts and permissions you configured on the Mac. The app is a client for that user-controlled environment; those systems and providers have their own retention and privacy practices. Do not send sensitive information unless you understand and accept their policies.

Permissions

Local-network access is used to discover and connect to a Mac Bridge. Optional photo-library access helps display recent photos for selection; selecting an attachment authorizes its transmission to your paired Mac. The app does not upload your entire photo library. You can manage permissions in iOS Settings.

Offline demo

Offline sample content is bundled with the app. Text typed in the demo remains in memory, is not submitted to a model or Bridge, and is discarded when the demo closes.

External links and support

Opening a help or privacy link requests the corresponding website through your browser. This support website is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process normal web request information under its privacy policy. Support requests submitted to GitHub are public and governed by GitHub's privacy policy. Do not include private conversations or pairing tokens.

Your choices and contact

You can stop a live connection, revoke photo/local-network permissions, delete saved connections, or remove the app and its data. AIHelper has no developer-managed account to delete. For privacy questions, contact the developer through the support page.

Changes

This page will be updated if these practices change.
